# Typical Hong Kong evidence for employment income (prototype notes)

- Payslip: employer name, employee name, pay period, basic salary, allowances / bonus, MPF employee
  contribution (usually 5% capped at HKD 1,500 per month), gross and net pay. Bank salary credits normally
  equal NET pay, so compare declared salary with GROSS / basic pay, not with the bank credit.
- Employment letter: position, start date, current salary; signed by HR on letterhead.
- Notice of salaries tax assessment (IRD): year of assessment runs 1 April - 31 March (e.g. 2024/25);
  "total income from employment" includes bonuses paid in that year.
- Companies Registry (ICRIS) search: CR number, incorporation date, status (Live / Dissolved / Deregistered),
  dissolution date, English and Chinese names, former names.
