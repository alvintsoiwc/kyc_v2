---
name: out-of-scope
description: Sources outside the MVP scope (business ownership, inheritance, investments, crypto): record the gap honestly.
---
# Procedure
1. Do not try to verify the source with the MVP tools. Optionally search_policy for what is normally expected.
2. Optionally lookup_company if a company is named (record its status), but registry existence alone does not
   evidence income from it.
3. Finish as unresolved: rationale "outside the automated scope; requires manual review", missing_evidence
   listing the documents an officer would normally request (e.g. audited accounts, dividend records,
   probate documents), open question for the officer.
